# Star Trek weapon sounds (TNG / DS9 / Voyager era)

57 of the 59 files come from the TrekCore sound archive, https://www.trekcore.com/audio/.
The other two, phasers/defiant_pulse_phaser.ogg and torpedoes/sto_photon_torpedo.ogg, come from
the Star Trek Online sound bank M_P_Player_Space_CB26.fsb that D supplied (from the Nexus Mods mod
"DISCO to DEFIANT Pulse Cannons", which puts the Defiant's pulse cannon sound into STO).
They were converted from MP3 to Ogg Vorbis (quality 5) and had leading silence cut. Nothing
else was changed. Each file keeps TrekCore's original name (minus "_clean").

## Copyright: read before you publish

These are the shows' own sound effects, and the copyright belongs to Paramount (CBS Studios).
TrekCore is a fan site, and nobody gives these out under a free licence.

The official Wesnoth add-on server accepts only GPL or Creative Commons assets
(https://wiki.wesnoth.org/Wesnoth:Copyrights), so an add-on that contains these files
can't go on that server. You can use them in a copy you play yourself or share privately.
To publish the add-on, swap in free sounds first (the earlier set is in ../free-sci-fi/).

## Contents

- phasers/: tng_phaser (and 2 to 11, in several lengths; tng_phaser4_top, 6, 8 and 9 are
  long sustained beams), tng_phaser_rifle (hand rifle), defiant_pulse_phaser (Defiant pulse cannon, from STO), borg_phaser, deflector_enterpriseb
- torpedoes/: tng_torpedo, tng_torpedo2 (5.9 s, long tail), tng_torpedo3, quantumtorpedoes,
  quantumtorpedoes2, klingon_torpedo, romulan_torpedo, sto_photon_torpedo (from STO)
- disruptors/: klingon_disruptor (1 to 5), klingon_weapon (1 to 3), romulan_disruptor1/2,
  cardassian_disruptor, tng_disruptor, tng_weapons/tng_weapons2 (mixed weapon fire)
- explosions/: smallexplosion1 to 3, largeexplosion1 to 4, tng_phaser_strike (phaser hitting a
  hull), shield_sizzle, force_field_hit, borg_struck_phaser/_2
- boarding/: klingon_transporter, klingon_transporter_2, tng_transporter1, tng_transporter3,
  voyager_transporter (for boarding parties; most are long and need trimming to 1 to 2 s)
- cloaking/: cloak_klingon, cloak_romulan(_2), decloak_romulan, decloak_birdofprey (14.8 s)

## Where to look for more

- Lower Decks: there's no public archive for it. The shows' sound designers haven't released
  their effects.
- Games (Armada I/II, Bridge Commander, Elite Force, Star Trek Online): their sounds sit inside
  the game files, so you'd have to pull them from a copy you own. They're copyrighted the same way.

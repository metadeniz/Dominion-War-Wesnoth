## Notes

- **Untextured sprites:** Thingiverse models are 3D-printing files (STL), which store shape only, so those sprites use a single tint.
- **maquis_full.stl:** the Thingiverse 2848310 print file splits the ship into two halves. The render joins them and removes the display stand.
- **ktinga_joined.stl:** the Thingiverse 3670957 bow and stern are separate print parts. The render lines them up on their shared cut face (bow raised 23.9 units).
- **Bajoran assault vessel:** no downloadable model exists, so the model was built here from the orthographic reference views (top, bottom, side, front). The shape is ours. Builder: `src/visualhull.py`. The light and dark pattern was first sampled from that reference picture, whose source is unknown, so confirm it or repaint before release. It is now repainted bronze-brown from the colours of a reference render D supplied, keeping the original light and dark pattern (`tools/recolor_vc.py`).
- **Hideki:** the model has no texture, so it is painted here by a shader preset (`hideki` in `src/page3.html`): tan plating with weathering, maroon claws and spine panels, ringed dome, amber windows, a blue-violet glow under the neck and red wingtip lights. The colours were judged by eye from DS9 stills, so no image from the show is copied.
- **Ferengi Marauder (Maquis leader):** Raul Mamoru's model from TrekMeshes. His readme allows free use in 3D renderings with the credit "Ferengi Marauder: Raul Mamoru - www.trekmeshes.ch" and forbids trading the file; it states no open licence. Its dark textures are rendered brighter (`br=4`).
- **Bajoran freighter:** the FBX's texture names say it comes from Star Trek Adversaries assets; Sketchfab lists it as CC BY. Its file carries the artist's own light, which lit the ship in every render; that light is now removed, so it shows its own rust-brown paint under our lighting.
- **octave767 models (Breen warship, Galor, Maquis fighter):** Sketchfab lists them as CC BY, but their texture files date from 2013 and look like an older game-mod asset, so the licence may not be the uploader's to give.
- **Maquis fighter lights:** the model's own lights were made brighter, with its blue strips slightly wider (`tools/perlights.py`, `tools/perwiden.py`). When shrunk to 72px, pixels that are already red or blue keep their colour instead of fading into the grey (`tools/sprite72.py`). Nothing from the original Peregrine (a Star Trek Online model D supplied) is used.
- **K'Vort:** the TTS B'rel model with its wings raised 65° into the cruise position (`tools/wingsup.py`), in the original olive paint with the dark grilles in the middle of the hull made darker so they stand out from the wings (`tools/darken_grilles.py`; `src/models/kvort_dark.obj` and `.png`).
- **Sprite sizes:** ships are sized by canon length on a compressed scale (visible size grows with length to the power 0.3). Each one stays inside its hex except the Jem'Hadar battleship, whose image is 96x96. The small craft keep their earlier size so they stay readable. Lengths and details are in `scale-notes.md`.
- **Excelsior, Akira, Intrepid and K't'inga:** morenostefanuto's textured Sketchfab models, under Sketchfab's Standard licence. That lets us use them in the game but not share them under an open licence, so the official add-on server may refuse them. They replaced untextured Thingiverse models; the K't'inga's size is set so its sprite covers the same area as the earlier one.
- **Nebula-class:** TrekMeshes' "Nebula class starship" (original Galaxy parts by Trevor Morris and Ralph Schoberth, Nebula kitbash by Rob Caves), converted here from its 1999 LightWave file and Amiga-format textures (`tools/lwob2obj.py`, `tools/ilbm.py`). TrekMeshes asks for credit to the authors; it states no licence, so the add-on server needs the authors' permission.
- **Maquis raider:** ZEOCMF's textured Condor-class from Sketchfab, CC BY-NC-ND, so preview only. Its size is set so the sprite covers the same area as the earlier Thingiverse model. Its colours are made bolder after the studio model: a neutral grey hull with stronger light and dark panels, gold panels, and glowing red engines and blue strip (`tools/raider_bold.py`). The ND licence does not allow sharing changed versions, so this stays a preview until the ship is replaced. It uses light rig 10 (D's favourite for it) instead of the default rig 14.
- **B'rel:** CrustyBread's green textured model from Sketchfab (CC BY), in the wings-down attack position. The K'Vort uses the olive TTS B'rel model.
- **Captured Constellation:** the TTS Constellation model with its hull repainted Maquis tan, while the blue nacelle glow keeps its Federation blue (`tools/const_cap.py`; `src/models/constellation_cap.png` and `_em.png`).
- **Nebula edits:** the yellow saucer windows are removed and the rear pod's yellowish ribs and trim are repainted light grey (converted model folder `obj_nowin_grey`).
- **Excelsior nacelles:** the model's saturated blue grilles on top of the nacelles are repainted in the nacelle's own grey, and the orange side bands are made the blue glow seen on screen (`Excelsior_v3.obj`/`.mtl`, made by a script from the original files). The bright blue hull panels (neck, pylons and the top of the saucer) are now grey; the nacelle glow is unchanged (`Excelsior_v4.mtl`, `tools/excelsior_tone.py`).
- **Dreadnought:** MazzTer's Thingiverse model is untextured, so it is painted here by a shader preset (`dread` in `src/page3.html`). Thingiverse lists it as CC BY 4.0, but its description says personal use only, so ask the author before release.
- **Brightness:** the Akira, Excelsior and K't'inga textures are rendered darker and the Nebula brighter so they sit with the other ships of their faction (`br=` in each model path in `src/roster2.json`, `tools/setbr.py`).
- **D5 and Raptor:** 22nd-century ships from Enterprise, added at D's request although they are older than the rest of the roster. The D5 is wholock's Sketchfab model (CC BY); its file carries the artist's own light, which made the wing plates render pale; that light is now removed, so the hull is drawn at its normal brightness (`br=1.0`, earlier `br=0.1`). The Raptor is MajorStress's Sketchfab model (CC BY), with its glow maps used for the lights; its material file pointed at the author's own disk, so a fixed copy points at the supplied textures. morenostefanuto's D5 (Sketchfab Standard) was rendered for comparison and is not in the roster: `kl-d5-morenostefanuto.png`.
- **Keldon:** calamity_si's textured model from 3D Warehouse. The author asks to be contacted for permission before any use and to be credited as 'calamity_si', so get permission before release. The rest of the ship is ALM Works' Galor, and the textures are Drex Files schematic renders of the DS9 model. The white background around the schematic textures is filled with hull tan (`tools/fillbg.py`), and the yellow vents and red lights glow.
- **Lighting (sheet v41, candidate C):** one layout for every ship: a low key light from the right that casts shadows on the ship, a fill light from front left and a weak rim light from back left, with no overhead or sky light. Two numbers are set per ship from its 72px sprite: the fill grows until the light-to-shadow ratio reaches about 2.4:1 (3.6:1 for Klingon ships) or the sprite's contrast falls to a floor, and all lights are then scaled so the brightness lands near 88 (darker ships keep half their gap, brighter ones a quarter; limits 55 to 100). The ground shadow is cast from straight above (`light` in `src/roster2.json`; method in `design/lighting-study.md`; tools `src/strategies3.py`, `src/buildopt.py`). D's own picks keep their lights, with only the shadow fix and brightness rule added: the Maquis raider (rig 10), the Jem'Hadar battleship (rig 8) and the Danube runabout (option 5, with its blue nacelle strip and red caps made to glow, `models/runabout_em.png`). Per-ship choices by D (sheet v42): option 1 (sheet v40, rig 14soft) on Noggra's freighter, the B'rel, K't'inga, Negh'Var, Hideki, Galor, Keldon, Breen warship, Jem'Hadar battle cruiser and Defiant; option 3 (candidate A) on the K'Vort. The light setups of option 1, option 3 and C are kept for every ship (`light_alt` in `src/roster2.json`), so any ship can be re-rendered under any of them; the option sprites themselves are not kept.
- **Retired sprites** are not kept (D, 15 Nov); earlier versions can be re-rendered from the setups in `src/`.
